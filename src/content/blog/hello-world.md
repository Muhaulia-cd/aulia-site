Welcome. This is the first post on my site. I'm starting this space to share two things:

1. **What I'm building**: projects, experiments, and lessons from vibe coding.
2. **What I'm learning**: as an educator and developer, writing helps me think clearly.

## Why Astro?

I chose [Astro](https://astro.build) for a simple reason. It's fast, static by default, and lets me write posts in plain Markdown. Adding a new post is just dropping a `.md` file into a folder, with no database and no CMS.

## What's next

Expect posts about building with AI, teaching tech, and the occasional deep dive. If you want to follow along, check back here or reach out.

Thanks for reading.
